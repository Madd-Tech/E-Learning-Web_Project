About This Course

Artificial Intelligence is reshaping every industry, and AI Engineers are at the forefront of this revolution. This advanced course covers the full spectrum of AI engineering — from deep learning architectures and natural language processing to building and deploying production-grade AI applications powered by Large Language Models (LLMs).

You'll work with cutting-edge tools including PyTorch, Hugging Face Transformers, LangChain, and OpenAI APIs. By the end of this program, you'll be able to fine-tune models, build RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) systems, create AI agents, and deploy intelligent applications at scale.

What You'll Learn

  • Deep learning with PyTorch
  • Transformer architectures explained
  • NLP & text processing pipelines
  • LLM fine-tuning (LoRA, QLoRA)
  • RAG systems & vector databases
  • AI agents with LangChain
  • Prompt engineering best practices
  • AI ethics & responsible deployment
